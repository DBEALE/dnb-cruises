About the club

A proper community club since the early 1900s

Rickmansworth Bowls Club plays flat green bowls at Basing Gardens, just off Northway, a short walk from the town centre and the station. Men and women play together, and members range from people in their twenties to people in their nineties.

Some members play league and county competitions, some only come for a roll-up and a chat on a sunny afternoon, and most do a bit of both. You can be as competitive or as relaxed as you like.

The club is affiliated to the Watford & District Bowls Association, Hertfordshire Bowls and Bowls England, so members can enter county and national competitions.

  • Friendly roll-ups

    Informal games most afternoons in season. Just turn up and you'll be put in a rink.

  • League & friendly matches

    Mixed, men's and ladies' sides against clubs across Hertfordshire and the Chilterns.

  • Club competitions

    Singles, pairs and triples championships, plus handicap events so newer players have a real chance.

  • Social calendar

    Open days, a summer barbecue, quiz nights and a presentation dinner at the end of the season.

New to bowls?

Try it for free. No kit, no experience, no commitment.

Bowls is easy to start, gentle on the body and surprisingly addictive. Our qualified coaches run free taster sessions throughout the season.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Use the contact form below or come along to an open day. Tell us roughly when you're free.

  2. 2

    Turn up in flat shoes

    Any flat-soled trainers are fine to start. We'll lend you a set of woods in the right size.

  3. 3

    Learn the basics

    A coach covers stance, delivery and the rules in about an hour, then you'll play a few ends.

  4. 4

    Stay for tea

    Meet the members and decide if it's for you. Plenty of first-timers join the same week.
